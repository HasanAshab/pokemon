based on that:
1. give more priority to same type moves
2. 20% moves will be high power,
   50% will be mid power (status moves will also stay there even there power is 0) and
   rest 20% percent will be low power

Note: formula to detect high mid and low move for a level is:
  Low: move.basePower <= LVL
  Mid: LVL < move.basePower <= LVL * 2
  High: LVL * 2 < move.basePower <= (LVL * 2) + 10
Example: If pokemon level is 20
  Low: move.basePower <= 20
  Mid: 20 < move.basePower <= 40
  High: 40 < move.basePower <= 50
